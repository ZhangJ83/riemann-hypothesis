Ranking is deterministic and input-order independent; the 4096 seed and 48 triple limits explicitly make this a bounded heuristic. All three full theorem bodies, and the new helper, must be independently added as kernel-checked theorem declarations with allowed axioms only. No proof irrelevance is used as a structural equality test. The current prototype only aligns equal-arity telescopes with identical universe parameter lists; it is incomplete and may reject good candidates.
