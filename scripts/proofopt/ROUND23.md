Round 23 extends the Lean proof-value pass with:
- Open the leading lambda telescope of both actual proof expressions, align equal-arity binder types by substitution and definitional equality, and rename right local variables to left local variables. Exact original theorem types/universes are used for both reconstructed declarations.
- Up to four syntactically distinct disagreement pairs; repeated pairs reuse one hole. Substitute in de Bruijn index order and require exact reconstruction. Earlier holes may be abstracted out of the inferred types of later holes; retain the full aligned telescope. Nested binder anti-unification, nonaligned telescopes, and universe renaming are deliberately not supported.
- A fresh helper and both reconstructed complete proofs are individually checked by Lean and the axiom allowlist. All helper type/value and reconstructed proof references are replayed using round 22's closure model. Same pair selection and historical graph for controlled comparison.
- Compare the union of raw syntactic Expr nodes across the two complete declarations before and across the two reconstructed declarations plus helper after. This is local, unnormalized and includes the helper cost; a capped result is unknown. Search at most 16 structural candidates per pair and select the highest local diagnostic gain among certified candidates. This does not imply global DAG optimality. A bounded failure does not prove mathematical impossibility; caught trial exceptions may include resource errors.
- Exact-root fixtures exercise two independent holes, dependent hole types and identical open fragments, plus incompatible telescope and owner negatives. The regular bounded search is also tested. Audit requires consistent certificate/replay provenance and never credits model or local diagnostic gains to the flagship measurements.
